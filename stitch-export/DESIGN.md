## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a focused, cognitive sanctuary for university students, researchers, and self-directed scholars. Designed to reduce digital fatigue and cognitive overload, the system fuses calm minimalism with modern AI-workspace ergonomics inspired by research notebooks and conversational knowledge interfaces.

### Personality & Values
- **Scholarly Composure:** Peaceful, quiet, and deliberate. Interfaces recede so that ideas, sources, and synthesis remain central.
- **Intelligent Guidance:** Contextual, discreet, and refined. Accent tones feel deliberate rather than flashy or urgent.
- **Spacious Architecture:** Generous whitespace, calm structural dividers, and warm, readable typographic hierarchies that facilitate deep work and extended study sessions.

### Visual Style
- **Calm Neo-Editorial Minimal:** Pure whites, gentle pale grays, and soft lavender tints replace jarring darks and saturated hues.
- **Surface Elevation over Shadowing:** Hierarchy relies on distinct container tiers, hairline dividers, and micro-tonal shifts rather than heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette creates a serene, low-arousal canvas optimized for lengthy reading and dense writing workflows.

### Color Tokens & Assignments
- **Canvas (`#F8F9FC`):** The default application canvas. A gentle, cool porcelain tone that reduces glare compared to pure white.
- **Surface Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** Work surfaces, active document cards, reading panels, and editor canvases.
- **Surface Secondary / Container (`#F1F3F9`):** Sidebars, source lists, inactive tabs, and reference side-drawers.
- **Primary Accent (`#6558E8`):** A soft, intelligent violet/indigo used for active synthesis states, primary buttons, AI interactions, and selected tabs.
- **Subtle Accent Tint (`#F0EEFF`):** Background highlights, chip selection fills, active citation backgrounds, and soft callout badges.
- **Success / Verified (`#279B78`):** Factual confirmation badges, completed revision milestones, and citation validation flags.
- **Text Primary (`#20212A`):** Deep charcoal ink, providing crisp contrast without harsh black-on-white vibration.
- **Text Secondary (`#777B8A`):** Slate gray for metadata, timestamps, word counts, and placeholder text.
- **Border Subtle (`#E8E9F0`):** Single-pixel perimeter strokes that bound panels without creating visual noise.

## Typography

The typography couples the welcoming, balanced geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the clarity and neutrality of **Inter** for sustained reading and interface elements.

- **Line Height Rhythms:** Generous body line heights (`1.625rem` to `1.875rem`) guarantee that dense academic texts, citations, and analytical notes remain easy to scan and read.
- **Tracking & Proportion:** Headings utilize subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain composure, while compact labels use slight positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) for legibility in dense research sidebars.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace uses a fluid, multi-pane layout modeled after modern intellectual suites: a contextual source rail, a dominant center working editor/synthesizer, and an optional collapsible insight sidebar.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptive Rules
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 3-column architecture (Sources / Active Canvas / AI Synthesis) with fluid interior containers, fixed maximum content line widths (720px for prose reading), and 2rem outer canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** Split-view or canvas-dominant mode. Sidebars collapse into soft slide-over trays. Gutters reduce to 1rem.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with swipeable bottom sheets for citations and generative prompts. Outer margins tighten to 1rem.

## Elevation & Depth

To eliminate cognitive friction and visual noise, this design system avoids multi-stop, heavy drop-shadows. Depth is articulated purely through surface tonal contrast and hairline containment.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F8F9FC`. Baseline screen background.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Sidebars):** `#F1F3F9` bounded by 1px solid `#E8E9F0`. Zero shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Reading Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#F8F9FC`, delineated by a 1px solid `#E8E9F0` border and a whisper ambient shadow: `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(32, 33, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Citations & Context Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E8E9F0` border and a delicate atmospheric shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(101, 88, 232, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 33, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded contours that feel friendly, modern, and calm without appearing childish.

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for primary study boards, document viewers, and AI answer panes.
- **Inner Modules & Popovers (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) for prompt input fields, contextual action boxes, and citation blocks.
- **Interactive Controls & Chips (`rounded`):** `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, drop-down triggers, text inputs, and source tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#6558E8` with `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-transition on hover to `#5346D6`. No drop shadow. Height: 40px, padding: `0 1rem`, shape: `rounded` (8px).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent or `#F0EEFF` fill with `#6558E8` text. 1px border `#E8E9F0` when on white backgrounds.
- **Icon Actions:** 36x36px square with 8px radius, `#777B8A` glyphs, transitioning to `#20212A` with a `#F1F3F9` background on hover.

### Source & Category Chips
- Pill-style or slightly rounded tags (height: 28px). Background: `#F0EEFF` with `#6558E8` typography for active/filter states; `#F1F3F9` with `#777B8A` typography for inactive reference tags.
- Verified or corroborated sources include an embedded 12px checkmark using `#279B78`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F1F3F9`. Border: 1px `#E8E9F0`.
- Focus state: Subtle 1px `#6558E8` stroke with a 3px soft outer ring in `rgba(101, 88, 232, 0.15)`.
- Text: `#20212A` body-md, placeholder: `#777B8A`.

### Cards & Reading Panes
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E8E9F0` outline and `rounded-xl` border radius.
- Padding: `1.5rem` internally. Clean separators using `#E8E9F0` for header and footer metadata sections.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18x18px. Border: 1.5px `#E8E9F0` with `rounded` (4px for checkbox, full circle for radio).
- Checked State: `#6558E8` background fill with white checkmark/dot.

### Academic Citations & Notes
- Integrated footnote callout badges: 20px height, `#F0EEFF` background, `#6558E8` label-sm text.
- Expands on click into an ambient popover (Level 3 elevation) displaying source title, author, and quote snippet.
